## Featured Project: Timber Management System

A real family timber trading business ran on paper notebooks, then on three Excel versions. I analyzed the workflows, defined the requirements and business rules, and directed an AI-assisted build of an integrated web application that is used in daily operations.

**How I approached it**

1. **Understand:** mapped the real sales, purchasing, inventory, collections and cash workflows
2. **Define:** wrote a phased specification with business rules and acceptance criteria
3. **Direct:** guided the AI-assisted build phase by phase and reviewed each result
4. **Validate:** migrated real business data and tested the workflows

My role was business analysis and product ownership. The application code was generated with Google AI Studio (Gemini) from my specification.
